Just Steps Privacy Policy

Just Steps · 한국어

Last updated: October 13, 2026. This policy covers the Just Steps Android app, package com.humanlemon.simplesteps.

Contact for privacy questions: just-steps@humanlemon.dev.

Step data and its purpose

With your permission, Just Steps reads step-count totals from Health Connect to show today's count. If you enable history, it reads daily totals for the previous seven days when you open the history view. It does not write or delete Health Connect records, track phone sensors, or access other health data types.

The app does not transmit step data off your device. It has no account, advertising, analytics, or remote crash-reporting service. Data availability and timing depend on Health Connect and the apps that provide its records.

Optional ongoing notification and widget

The ongoing notification is off by default. When you turn it on, the app requests notification permission where required. On supported devices, it explains and separately requests Health Connect background-read access to update the notification when the app is not on screen. Declining background access still lets you check steps in the app; the notification then shows the last total read while the app was visible. You can stop the feature in app settings or with Stop display in the notification. The app does not restart the service after a reboot.

The widget is off by default and is added by you to your home screen. When enabled, it stores today's total, date, device time zone, and check time in the app's private local storage. The step count and its accessibility description are passed to Android's widget system and your chosen home screen app to display the widget on this device. The date, time zone, and check time are used to avoid showing stale records. The widget visibly shows only the number. The notification similarly uses Android's notification system. These displays can be seen by people who can view your screen, subject to your device's lock-screen settings.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Display settings stay in the app's private storage until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Today's current reading and the optional seven-day history are held in memory; the app clears the displayed history when you leave the app or turn history off.

The widget cache is cleared when the widget is turned off, or when the app detects a revoked step permission, a failed step read, an expired date, or a changed time zone. Detection requires the app or widget code to run, so clearing is not guaranteed at the instant a permission or date changes. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes its local settings and cache. This app excludes its settings and cache from Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup.

Removing local app data does not remove the original records in Health Connect or a source app. Manage those records and revoke Just Steps access in Health Connect settings or the app that created them.

Use, sharing, and security

Just Steps uses step data only for the displays you choose. It does not sell it or use it for advertising, user profiling, eligibility decisions, or research. The developer does not receive your step records. Apart from the local Android notification and home screen displays described above, the app has no data-sharing or export feature.

Local settings and cache use Android's app-private storage and device security. The app does not add a separate encryption layer to that storage. Protect your device with a screen lock and review which home screen app and notification settings you use.

Email, external pages, and policy changes

If you choose to email support, we use the email address and information you send to answer your request. Please do not send health records. Email is processed by the email providers involved. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request or meet a legal obligation. You may request deletion of support correspondence by writing to the contact address above, subject to applicable legal obligations. The app does not automatically attach step records to email.

This privacy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit a GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes, whether or not you are signed into GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. Your browser may also process ordinary connection information under its own policy. The app does not attach step data to the link.
